POURCOST
Multi-Bar COGS Tracker — First-Time Setup
STEP 1 OF 4
WELCOME TO POURCOST
You're setting up PourCost for two bars — Desert Hawk and The Low Horse.

Each bar will have its own completely separate data: weekly COGS, purchases, price history, and product maps. You can switch between them instantly, or view a combined report.

All data lives permanently in a single Google Sheet you own, accessible from any device. This setup takes about 5–8 minutes.
STEP 2 OF 3
CREATE YOUR SPREADSHEET
  1. Go to sheets.google.com and click Blank.
  2. Name it PourCost (or anything you like).
  3. Click Extensions → Apps Script.
  4. Delete everything in the editor and paste the script from your setup instructions.
  5. Click Deploy → New Deployment → Web App.
  6. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone.
  7. Click Deploy, authorize, and copy the Web App URL.
STEP 3 OF 3
PASTE YOUR SCRIPT URL
Paste the Web App URL you got after deploying your Apps Script:
PourCost will use this URL to read and write your sheet data. It's stored only in this browser.
